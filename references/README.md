# References

This directory contains the bibliographic resources, research materials, and supporting literature consulted during the development of the **Anteprimicosm Framework**.

The purpose of this directory is to document intellectual influences, provide appropriate attribution, and encourage readers to explore related works in philosophy, metaphysics, cosmology, ontology, and the philosophy of science.

## Purpose

This directory serves to:

- Record works that have informed the development of the framework.
- Distinguish established philosophical literature from original concepts introduced in this project.
- Maintain transparency regarding sources and influences.
- Provide readers with references for further study.

## Categories

The reference collection may include:

- Classical Philosophy
- Metaphysics
- Ontology
- Cosmology
- Philosophy of Science
- Logic
- Mathematics and Foundations
- Theology and Comparative Thought (where relevant)
- Scientific Literature
- Historical Sources

## Citation Style

References will generally follow a consistent academic citation style (such as APA, Chicago, or MLA). The preferred citation format may be standardized as the project develops.

## Original Contributions

The **Anteprimicosm Framework** introduces original terminology and conceptual structures developed within this repository. While the framework may engage with existing philosophical traditions, any newly defined concepts should be understood as original contributions unless explicitly attributed to prior works.

## Living Bibliography

This directory functions as a living bibliography. New references may be added, updated, or reorganized as research progresses and the framework continues to develop.
