# Anteprimicosm Framework

> *A Philosophical Framework for Exploring the Conceptual Foundations of Describable Reality.*
## Framework Overview

![Anteprimicosm Framework](figures/anteprimicosm-framework.png)
## Welcome

The **Anteprimicosm Framework** is an original philosophical framework that explores the conceptual conditions under which describable reality becomes meaningful.

Rather than proposing an alternative scientific theory or cosmological model, this framework investigates questions concerning ontology, metaphysics, conceptual emergence, and the limits of human description.

Its purpose is to provide a structured philosophical vocabulary for discussing the deepest conceptual foundations of reality while remaining compatible with established scientific inquiry.

## Vision

To develop an open philosophical framework that encourages thoughtful discussion on the conceptual foundations of existence and the emergence of describable reality.

## Disclaimer

The Anteprimicosm Framework is presented as a speculative philosophical framework.

It is not an established scientific theory and does not claim empirical verification. Its purpose is to encourage philosophical inquiry into the conceptual foundations of reality while respecting the methods and findings of established scientific disciplines.
