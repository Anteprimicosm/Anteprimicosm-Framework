# Anteprimicosm Framework

## Version 1.0

**Status:** First Edition

This release represents the first complete edition of the Anteprimicosm Framework.

It includes the Preface, Introduction, twelve core chapters, Conclusion, Afterword, Glossary, and References and Further Reading.

Version 1.0 establishes the foundational concepts, terminology, and philosophical structure of the framework. Future editions may refine, expand, or clarify these ideas while preserving the framework's central objective:

> *To explore the conceptual foundations under which reality becomes meaningfully describable.*
